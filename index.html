Lanevora: Traffic Racer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026. Published by Webillium. Contact: kelneronline@gmail.com.

Game progress

Scores, coins, vehicle unlocks and selections are saved locally on your device. Lanevora does not require an account and does not operate an online leaderboard. Clearing application data or uninstalling may remove this progress.

Advertising

Lanevora uses Google AdMob for banners on menus and results, interstitial advertising between runs, and optional rewarded advertising for a revive or extra coins. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, advertising interactions and diagnostic information to deliver, secure and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Your choices

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform displays privacy choices before requesting ads. Open Privacy, then Ad Privacy Choices in the game to revisit available choices. Rewarded ads are optional. Interstitial ads may appear between completed runs; they do not interrupt active driving. Advertising controls are also available in Android settings.

Permissions and audience

The game does not request precise location, contacts, microphone or camera access. It is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information through gameplay. Contact us if you believe information about a child has been provided.

Retention and requests

Local game data remains until cleared. Advertising data retention is governed by Google's policies and applicable consent settings. For privacy questions or data requests, contact kelneronline@gmail.com. We will update this policy when our data practices change.
